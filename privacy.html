Dateborne Privacy Policy

Effective October19,2026. Developer: Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir. Dateborne is an on-device photo date editor for iPhone and iPad.

Your photographs

Dateborne reads only photos you select through system Photos or Files controls. It processes JPEG/HEIC still images locally and creates separate copies. It does not upload your photos, embedded dates or filenames to a developer server and does not modify your original photos or documents.

Working copies, thumbnails, exports and verification receipts are temporary files inside the app. Starting a new session or launching a new app process clears the working session. Save exports you want to keep before closing the app or starting over. Date presets are saved locally until you delete them or remove the app. Your own system backup settings may apply to presets.

Photos permission and sharing

Photo-library add permission is requested only when you choose to add new copies to Photos. The app creates new assets and sets their library creation date. It does not edit existing assets. Sharing, saving to Files or adding to Photos sends copies only to destinations you choose. Those destinations, including iCloud Photos or cloud storage providers, use their own privacy policies and may synchronize data.

Purchases

Apple handles Lifetime Pro purchases, prices, transaction verification and restoration through StoreKit. Dateborne does not receive payment-card information and does not transmit receipts or purchase details to a developer backend.

No tracking or content analytics

Dateborne has no account, advertising SDK, tracking, developer analytics upload, external AI service or product backend. We do not collect your personal data through the app. Apple may provide aggregate App Store reporting and crash information under Apple's services and your device settings.

Support

If you contact hello@arvinify.com, your email service sends the information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request. You do not need to send private photographs for ordinary support.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected in this policy with a new effective date. Questions: hello@arvinify.com · arvinify.com.
